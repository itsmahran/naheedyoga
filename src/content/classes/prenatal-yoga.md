A gentle and supportive practice designed specifically for expectant mothers. These sessions focus on relieving pregnancy-related discomfort, building strength for labor, and using breathwork to connect deeply with your baby. With our background in Naturopathy, we provide a safe environment to help you manage stress and physical changes with grace and mindfulness.
